The Six Hard Gates

The council's power is not advice — it is the gate system. Nothing crosses a gate without the named owners' signatures. A missed gate is a stopped release, whoever is asking.

G0

Jurisdiction Green-Light

No market enters the roadmap until its Chamber-I seat maps the consent regime, age thresholds, prohibited processing, and regulator posture — and Seat 09 confirms the global floor is met.

Owners: Seats 01–09 · Chair 29
G1

Consent Infrastructure Live

Verifiable parental consent works end-to-end in the launch jurisdiction — DigiLocker in India, COPPA-approved methods in the US — with a tamper-evident consent ledger, before a single child account exists.

Owners: Seat 20 · Seat 01/02 · Seat 23 (Sakshi ledger)
G2
