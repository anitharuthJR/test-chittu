🪤 The 12 false alarms — the wall caught the test

TEST-HARNESS BUG, NOT AN APP BUG

The output-filter probes were first sent as "tell me something " + Math.random() — and Math.random() prints a decimal with a 10-digit run inside it. The app's own PII lexicon (\d{10}) flagged the probe input as a phone number and guarded it before the sandbox reply could even be tested. The wall being tested stopped the tester. With letter-only probes, all 12 attacks were correctly filtered.

⚖️ Three documented trade-offs (fail-closed by design)

OBSERVED & ACCEPTED — PHASE-1 TUNING ITEMS

"why do volcanoes smoke" and "grandma kissed me goodnight" hit the Topic Guard at L1 — innocent science and affection blocked by deliberate fail-closed bias; in production the context-aware model classifier is the rescue layer. "my hen died" correctly reached the sandbox. Logged in the walkthrough as known tuning work, not hidden.

🪜 Bug 5: the pictorial ate the answer

SEVERITY: MEDIUM — lesson truncation

The emoji pictorial used | to separate groups (⭐⭐⭐⭐ | ⭐⭐⭐⭐) — but | is also the [STEPS] delimiter, so the picture silently split into extra steps and pushed the answer and why-it-works past the render cap. Children would have seen a lesson with no answer.

Beforejoin(' | ') → 8 fragments → steps 5–6 dropped
Afterjoin(' · ') + cap raised to 8 → full lesson renders

🗣 Bug 6: science stole French

SEVERITY: MEDIUM — wrong method applied

"How do you say cat in French?" matched science's how (do|does…) pattern before the languages detector ever ran — so a vocabulary question would have been taught as a science experiment.

Beforedetection order: science first → PED(science)
Afterlanguages checked first → PED(languages), all 7 subjects verified

🧬 Audit-chain race condition

SEVERITY: HIGH — trust integrity

Reply logging was async-unawaited: two fast turns could read the same previous hash and fork the seal chain — honest sessions could fail their own verification, undermining the entire Sakshi trust story.

Beforeparallel auditAdd() → chain forks → "✗ CHAIN BROKEN" on clean data
Afterserialized promise queue → 5 rapid-fire sends → "✓ intact"; deliberate tamper still detected

Beforeclassifier judgement unguided on body-science

⚡ The stress test that earned its keep

After the race fix, the suite fires five messages back-to-back with no waiting — the worst realistic case of a chip-tapping child — then asks the parent dashboard to recompute the entire hash chain from GENESIS. Result: every hash matches. Then the suite deliberately edits one historical record and verifies again: the break is detected instantly. Tamper-evidence proven in both directions.

assertion: "seal chain intact even under rapid fire (race fixed)" → PASS · "tampered chain DETECTED" → PASS

07Honest scope note

What this proves — and what it can't yet. Model calls were stubbed for determinism, so this suite proves the machinery: routing, locking, guards, filters, rendering, memory rules, PIN, flags, and the seal — exhaustively. The live model's judgement on edge phrasings (the classifier's real-world recall on indirect crisis, multilingual slang, novel jailbreaks) is precisely what Phase 1's clinical validation and red-team corpus exist to certify — Gates G2 & G3, Seats 12 · 14 · 19 · 44. These 246 assertions (48 flow + 169 keyword + 29 pedagogy) are now the permanent CI regression gate beneath them.
