The routing principle: the more sensitive or exact the moment, the less AI is used — and where AI is used, the router is provider-agnostic: every AI cell has a named alternate from the Aug-2026 landscape (Gemini 2.5/LearnLM for pedagogy, Merlyn Origin for self-hosted curriculum-only answering, Orca-Math/MathGPT for math specialisation, TinyStories-class SLMs for offline stories, Ello/Amira-class ASR for reading). Pedagogy rides above routing: whichever engine answers, the same teaching stack applies — subject method (12a), CPA maths (12b), universal engine (14: YOUR-TURN tasks, growth mindset) — deterministically in the maths engine, by prompt in the sandbox, visible in the trace as · PED(subject). Two standing conditions (Seat 10): every provider's minors-use terms must clear legal review before routing a single child turn to it; and the scripted spine, crisis script, and guards are never outsourced to any vendor model, ever.

  


  
  

    
D4Crisis spine — detection to parent unlock

      

    

    

    
Four detectors, one script, one exit: lexicon, classifier, sandbox escalation, and vision all converge on the same scripted response with the country's verified helplines — and only a parent's acknowledgment in the dashboard unlocks normal chat. The exit is a human, not a keyword.

  


  
  

    
D5Parent flow — PIN gate to full control

      

    

    

    
Two honest asymmetries: the transcript view splits by age (full for 4–12, topics-only for teens — and the child knows either way), and the seal-verify button lets the parent mathematically prove no record was edited, including records of their own access.
